Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

AdminEase ("we," "us," "our") provides administrative support services to clients in New Jersey and New York. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us — by phone, email, text, or WhatsApp — and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Your name, phone number, and email address, when you provide them to us.
  • The content of messages you send us by phone, text, WhatsApp, or email, so we can respond to and keep track of your requests.

How we use it

We use this information solely to provide administrative support services you've requested — creating and tracking tasks, following up on requests, and communicating with you about their status. We do not use your information for marketing, and we do not sell or share it with third parties, except as needed to carry out the specific task you've asked us to help with (for example, contacting a vendor on your behalf).

Text and WhatsApp messaging

If you text or WhatsApp AdminEase, that message and our replies are used only to manage and respond to your request. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of text/WhatsApp communication at any time by replying STOP, or get help by replying HELP.

Data retention

We keep records of client communications for as long as needed to provide our services and to maintain a history of completed work, and delete or de-identify information on request.
